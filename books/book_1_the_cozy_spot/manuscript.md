# The Cozy Spot

Book 1 · 24 pages · Landscape page 11" × 8.5" (16:9)
Cast: @munchie, @lunie, @bast
Lesson: Sharing one cozy spot, waiting with patience, and softening big feelings

Dynamic picture-book pacing (Bluey action beats, Oliver Jeffers whitespace). Narrative text is typeset in Photoshop and appears only where the story needs it. Art is generated as separate assets, then assembled on the landscape page. Do not bake words into the images.

**Layouts**
- **Full-Bleed Spread:** one image fills the page.
- **2-Panel Sequence (vertical split):** left panel and right panel.
- **2-Panel Sequence (horizontal split):** top panel and bottom panel.
- **4-Panel Action Grid:** four panels in a 2×2 grid, read left to right, top to bottom.
- **Vignette / Spot Art:** one image centered in clean whitespace.
