# Munching Cats Character Abilities & Roster

## Protagonists
- **Munchie (Leader):** Rallies the team, makes bold moves, expresses big emotions, driven by hangry motivation.
- **Lunie (Spy):** Reaches high places, moves without a sound, solves visual puzzles, quiet observer.
- **Bast (Guardian):** Grounds the group, anchors chaos, provides physical and emotional warmth.
- **Teetee (Heart):** Connects team feelings, mediates conflicts, emotional conscience.
- **Moitee (Strategist):** Formulates step-by-step plans, anticipates obstacles, calculated problem-solver.
- **Crunchie (Enforcer):** Direct power, pushes through physical barriers, gets things done quickly.
- **Chewie (Chef/Alchemist):** Mixes treats, invents unexpected solutions, playful trial-and-error.

## Antagonists, Neutrals & Aliens
- **Alastor (Mushroom Emperor):** Main antagonist; spreads fungal spores and chaos, forces team cooperation.
- **The Chaotic Chihuahua:** Corporate supervisor / Boss barrier; loud, demanding, creates comedic deadlines.
- **The 3 Aliens:**
  - **Hugh Man:** Inquisitive alien attempting (and failing) to blend in as a normal human.
  - **Fun Guy:** Goofy, whimsical alien easily swayed by cat treats and silly games.
  - **Tom:** Quiet alien technician who introduces absurd cosmic gadgets into ordinary situations.
