# Target Audience & Publishing Specifications

- **Target Age:** 4–8 Years (Preschool to Grade 2)
- **Orientation:** Horizontal Landscape (11" x 8.5")
- **Trim Size:** 11" x 8.5" (16:9 Aspect Ratio)
- **Page Count:** 24 Pages (Standard Picture Book)
- **Pedagogy:** Social-Emotional Learning (Sharing, Emotional Regulation)
- **Language Structure:** Present Tense, 3rd Person POV
- **Text Placement:** Dedicated top pastel banner (separate from image scene)
